Start with the problem you need to solve

Send a short project brief to InertialRange Labs AB or book an intro call. Get a suggested next step toward a scoped AI engineering engagement.

Send a brief

A few lines are enough:

  • What are you building or already running?
  • What decision, problem, or delivery do you need help with?
  • If known, what platform and timeline are involved?

Start an email with these prompts Book an intro call

The email button opens a draft in your mail app. You can also write directly to farrukh.nauman@inertialrange.com. A high-level description is enough for the first conversation.

What happens next

I usually reply within 1–2 business days with whether the work fits, any questions needed to scope it, and a suggested next step. If there is a fit, we work toward a written proposal with deliverables, acceptance criteria, and price or rate. Work begins after we agree the scope and contract.

If you are still choosing a starting point, compare the four offers.

Contracting details

InertialRange Labs AB is a Swedish Aktiebolag based in Linköping. Engagements can be contracted directly or through your consultant broker, with invoicing in SEK or EUR. NDAs and access requirements can be agreed during scoping.
